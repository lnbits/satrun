# Sat Run

Sat Run is an LNbits game where you climb a neon obstacle course and outrun
an ever-rising void. Find your rhythm, time your jumps and see how high you can go.

An LNbits user creates a circuit and shares its public player link. Players
choose a callsign and enter the course. Every day brings a new layout, with a
daily leaderboard for each circuit. Starting a new run is always free.

## Game flow

1. Enter the circuit with one life and start climbing.
2. Dodge lasers and flying discs, cross moving platforms and time your jumps
   between rotating slabs.
3. Reach checkpoints to save your place. By default, there is one every 10 floors.
4. Collect extra-life pickups or a five-second flight boost along the way.
5. After a fall, use an extra life or pay the displayed price to resume from your
   last checkpoint. You can also start a fresh run for free.

A flight boost lifts you upward while you steer; keep clear of hazards.
Checkpoint resumes preserve your progress and count toward the daily leaderboard.
Each payment buys one resume with one life.

## Daily leaderboard

Open **Daily Leaderboard** from the lobby to see how your run compares.
The highest floor wins, with the fastest time to that floor breaking ties.
Time carries over when you resume from a checkpoint.

The course and daily rankings reset at **00:00 UTC**. **My Records** tracks your
personal bests, including practice runs. Pausing a daily run turns it into a
practice run.

## Controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Jump | Space |
| Rotate camera | Drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel |
| Reset camera | C |
| Pause / resume | Escape |
| Restart after a fall | R |

Touch devices have an on-screen joystick and jump button. Sound and fullscreen
controls are available in the game.

## Get started

Enable **Sat Run** in LNbits and open it to create a circuit. Choose the checkpoint
spacing, difficulty, resume price and receiving wallet, then share the player link.

For a quick practice run, open `/ext/satrun/play`. Circuit settings are available
at `/ext/satrun`.
